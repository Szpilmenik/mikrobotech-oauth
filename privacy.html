Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Mikrobotech AI is a private, single-user OAuth client. It is used by its owner, and only by its owner, to access data belonging to the same Google Account that authorises it.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's explicit consent, the client may read and modify Google Calendar events, Gmail messages, Drive files and Contacts belonging to that account.

How data is stored

Data is processed locally on the owner's own computer. OAuth credentials are stored in a local file readable only by the owner's system user. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared or transferred to any third party. No advertising or analytics services are used.

Retention and deletion

Locally cached data can be deleted by the owner at any time; access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
